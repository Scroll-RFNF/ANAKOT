Frequently asked questions

How do I find a suitable university?

Visit the Universities page and choose between universities in Cambodia or international universities. Compare the major, study duration, language of instruction and tuition before shortlisting an option.

Where can I find scholarship opportunities?

Open the Scholarships page. Review each opportunity's level, eligibility and funding details, then follow the official program link for current deadlines and application instructions.

Are the tuition fees and deadlines guaranteed?

No. Fees, programmes, eligibility rules and deadlines can change. Always confirm information on the university or scholarship provider's official website before making a decision or applying.

What documents should I prepare?

Common documents include academic transcripts, certificates, identification, recommendation letters, a passport, language-test results, a statement of purpose and proof of work experience when required.

Can Anakot submit an application for me?

Anakot provides information and preparation guidance. You must submit your application through the official university or scholarship portal, unless an approved representative or provider gives you different instructions.

How can I report incorrect information?

Send the page name, the incorrect detail and a link to the official source through the Contact page. This helps the information be reviewed and corrected.

How should I compare two universities?

Compare accreditation, campus location, recognized degrees, admission requirements, total annual cost, available accommodation and graduate outcomes. Do not compare tuition alone.

Do I need to use an agent or consultant?

Not necessarily. Most official applications can be submitted directly. If you use an agency, confirm that it is authorized by the university or scholarship provider and never send it passwords or original documents.

How can I identify a scholarship scam?

Warning signs include guaranteed awards, requests for upfront fees, payment through personal accounts, unofficial email addresses and pressure to decide quickly. Verify the provider through its official website and contact details.

What if I need a visa or language test?

Check each institution's minimum language score and the destination country's visa rules. Arrange tests and visa documents only after you understand the current official requirements and timelines.

Can Anakot guarantee admission or funding?

No. Anakot provides information for planning and is not an admissions office, scholarship provider or visa authority. The final decision belongs to the institution or awarding organization.

Prepare the right documents

Identity

Identification and passport

Keep clear copies of your ID card and passport. Check the passport validity period required for travel or visa processing.

Academics

Transcripts and certificates

Prepare certified copies when requested. Ask whether transcripts must arrive directly from your school or be translated.

English

Language test results

Check accepted tests, minimum scores and test validity. Confirm the institutional code before booking or sending a report.

References

Recommendation letters

Choose teachers or supervisors who know your work. Give them the deadline, your goals and the letter format early.

Personal

Statement and resume

Explain your academic goals, relevant experience and future plans. Proofread carefully and follow each program's word limit.

Financial

Funding evidence

Collect bank statements or sponsor letters only if the official application requires them. Never alter or present false evidence.

Planning timeline

12–18 months before

Explore and shortlist

Compare programmes and institutions. Note admission, language, funding and visa requirements for every option.

9–12 months before

Prepare tests and documents

Request transcripts and references, prepare a statement of purpose and complete required language tests.

6–9 months before

Apply and track

Submit ahead of deadlines. Save confirmations and respond quickly to document or interview requests.

After acceptance

Confirm and plan

Verify the offer, funding terms and conditions. Begin scholarship acceptance, housing and visa procedures.

Application checklist

1

Check eligibility

Confirm citizenship, degree level, grades, work experience, age and language requirements on the official source.

2

Choose your options

Compare at least two to three programmes using available majors, location, cost, career goals and entry requirements.

3

Prepare your documents

Request transcripts and references early. Scan clear copies and check whether official translations or notarization are required.

4

Review the application

Check names, dates and document formats. Ask a trusted person to proofread essays or statements before submission.

5

Apply before the deadline

Submit through the official application portal and save a screenshot or confirmation email for your records.

6

Track follow-up requests

Monitor your email and application account for document requests, interview invitations or acceptance decisions.
