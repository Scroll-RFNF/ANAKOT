Featured opportunities

Australia Masters / PhD

Australia Awards Scholarship

For Cambodian citizens pursuing study in Australia that supports Cambodia's development. The 2027 intake information is available through the official program.

  • Coverage: Full tuition, living costs, travel, health cover and academic support
  • Requirement: Two years' full-time work experience; residence in Cambodia for the required period
  • English: IELTS 6.0 for most coursework masters; 6.5 for research masters or PhD
Official program Apply in OASIS
United Kingdom One-year masters

Chevening Scholarships

A UK government scholarship for future leaders, professionals and innovators pursuing a one-year taught master's degree.

  • Coverage: Full funding for an eligible UK one-year master's degree
  • Requirement: Undergraduate degree and at least 2,800 hours of work experience
  • Commitment: Return to Cambodia for at least two years after the scholarship
Cambodia page Check eligibility
Europe Joint masters

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

Study in an international consortium across at least three institutions and countries in Europe or beyond. Scholarships may cover participation, travel and living costs.

  • Duration: 1 to 2 academic years
  • Requirement: A bachelor's degree or equivalent, or final-year bachelor status
  • Application: Apply directly to each master's programme, usually between October and January
Official European Commission guide Find a programme
Japan Undergraduate / Graduate

MEXT Scholarship

The Japanese Government MEXT scholarship is available through Japanese embassies and selected universities. Awards are offered for undergraduate, research and sometimes other study levels.

  • Coverage: Monthly allowance, tuition exemption and round-trip travel may be included
  • Requirement: Eligibility varies by study level, degree and recommended application route
  • Application: Normally apply through the Embassy of Japan in Cambodia or a participating university
Official Study in Japan guide Cambodia embassy programme
South Korea Bachelors / Masters / PhD

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)

GKS is a Korean Government scholarship for international students pursuing undergraduate or graduate degree study in South Korea.

  • Coverage: Tuition, living allowance, settlement support, health insurance and travel may be included
  • Requirement: Age and academic eligibility depend on degree level and selected application track
  • Application: Apply through the embassy track or a participating Korean university, according to the annual notice
Official GKS guide Study in Korea portal
United States Graduate study

Fulbright Foreign Student Program

A competitive U.S. government-funded programme that supports selected international graduates with study or research in the United States.

  • Coverage: The award typically includes tuition and approved living, travel or study support
  • Requirement: Requires relevant academic credentials, English proficiency and a competitive application
  • Application: Cambodian applicants should confirm availability and instructions with the U.S. Embassy
Official Fulbright programme U.S. Embassy Cambodia
Germany Postgraduate

DAAD EPOS

Postgraduate development-related courses in Germany for graduates with professional experience from eligible developing countries.

  • Coverage: Scholarship support may include a monthly allowance, insurance, travel and other benefits
  • Requirement: A relevant degree, professional experience and selected EPOS-eligible course
  • Application: Apply directly to each participating university under its DAAD EPOS notice
Official DAAD scholarships Search programmes
France Masters / PhD

Eiffel Excellence Scholarship

A French government scholarship programme for outstanding international students applying to selected master's and doctoral courses.

  • Coverage: Monthly allowance and benefits such as travel, insurance and programme support may apply
  • Requirement: Apply to a participating French institution; age limits differ by study level
  • Application: Submit the institutional application; host institutions nominate candidates
Official Eiffel guide Campus France
Netherlands Undergraduate / Masters

NL Scholarship

Partial scholarships from Dutch higher-education institutions for international students from outside the European Economic Area.

  • Coverage: Usually offers a contribution toward study costs rather than full tuition
  • Requirement: Admission to an eligible Dutch institution and meeting that institution's nationality rules
  • Application: Applications, deadlines and award amounts vary by Dutch host institution
Official NL Scholarship guide Study in NL
Asia-Pacific Postgraduate

ADB-Japan Scholarship

The Asian Development Bank-Japan Scholarship supports students from ADB developing member countries to study in selected postgraduate programmes.

  • Coverage: Tuition, living allowance, insurance, travel and research support may be included
  • Requirement: At least two years' relevant work experience and admission to a designated programme
  • Application: Apply to a participating academic institution and identify the ADB-JSP application process
Official ADB-JSP programme Participating institutions
International Masters

Rotary Peace Fellowship

Rotary funds selected postgraduate programmes for professionals committed to advancing peace and conflict resolution worldwide.

  • Coverage: Tuition, fees, room and board, travel and field-study expenses may be included
  • Requirement: A minimum of three years of relevant experience is required for the master's track
  • Application: Apply through a Rotary district or a Rotary Peace Center, as stated in the annual guide
Official Rotary fellowship Application process
United States Undergraduate

Gates Scholarship

A highly competitive, full-cost scholarship for outstanding, minority, high school seniors in the United States. It is not automatically open to Cambodian applicants.

  • Coverage: Cost of attendance not covered by another financial-aid source may be funded
  • Requirement: U.S. high-school status, residency or citizenship rules and unmet financial need apply
  • Application: Follow the official annual application process and verify all nationality eligibility rules
Official Gates Scholarship Check eligibility
New Zealand Diploma / Bachelors / Postgraduate

Manaaki New Zealand Scholarship

New Zealand Government scholarships for eligible students from selected partner countries who will contribute to their nation's development.

  • Coverage: Tuition, living allowance, travel, insurance and establishment support may be included
  • Requirement: Eligibility, work experience and available fields vary by applicant country
  • Application: Cambodia availability should be confirmed on the official country and eligibility pages
Manaaki scholarships Cambodia eligibility
Singapore Undergraduate / Graduate

Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)

A competitive award by Singaporean research institutions for international students pursuing PhD research in science and engineering.

  • Coverage: Monthly stipend and tuition support are provided, with a programme application and award period
  • Requirement: Strong academic record and acceptance or match with a participating supervisor or institution
  • Application: Apply through the official SINGA application portal and nominate preferred host institutions
Official SINGA programme How to apply
